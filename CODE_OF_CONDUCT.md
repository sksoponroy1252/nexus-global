# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Nexus Global is committed to providing an open, respectful, inclusive, and constructive community for everyone who participates in the project.

We welcome contributions from people with different backgrounds, experiences, skill levels, and perspectives.

Our goal is to maintain a professional environment where people can learn, collaborate, experiment, and build together.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Communicate constructively.
- Welcome different perspectives and technical approaches.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Accept reasonable criticism gracefully.
- Focus discussions on ideas and technical problems.
- Respect other contributors' time and effort.
- Protect private and sensitive information.
- Help maintain a welcoming environment for new contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or hateful conduct.
- Threats or deliberate intimidation.
- Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Malicious or abusive behavior toward contributors.
- Deliberately submitting harmful code or security vulnerabilities without responsible disclosure.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make the community unsafe or hostile.

## Technical Discussions

Technical disagreement is expected and can be valuable.

When disagreements occur:

1. Focus on the technical issue rather than the individual.
2. Provide evidence and reasoning where possible.
3. Keep discussions constructive.
4. Avoid personal attacks or hostile language.
5. Respect the final decision of maintainers when a decision is required.

## Security

Do not publicly disclose sensitive security vulnerabilities, credentials, API keys, passwords, or private information.

Security issues should be reported according to the project's SECURITY.md policy.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may take appropriate action when behavior violates these guidelines, including:

- Asking participants to stop inappropriate behavior.
- Removing inappropriate content.
- Closing discussions or issues.
- Restricting participation.
- Removing access to project resources when necessary.

Actions will be considered based on the severity and context of the situation.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainer.

Please provide:

- A description of the incident.
- Relevant links or context.
- Any available evidence.
- Information about people involved, when relevant.

Do not publicly expose sensitive personal information.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, discussions, pull requests, documentation, and other official Nexus Global community spaces.

## Good-Faith Participation

Nexus Global encourages good-faith collaboration.

Mistakes, disagreements, and beginner questions are normal parts of open-source development. Contributors should be given reasonable opportunities to learn and improve.

## Acknowledgement

This Code of Conduct is inspired by common open-source community standards, including principles found in the Contributor Covenant.

Nexus Global may adapt these guidelines as the project and community evolve.
